What it measures

A score for the layer that decides retention.

The Participation Quotient evaluates the five systems of Participation Systems™ — Orientation, Continuity, Participation, Trust and Fandom — and returns a single, honest picture of where attention is leaking and where belonging is forming.

It isn't a personality quiz and it isn't a vanity metric. It's a diagnostic built from two decades of shipping participation at companies like Marvel and Microsoft, and advising the platforms defining what comes next.

Fifteen minutes in. A clear read on what's holding retention back — and the first moves that change it.

The five archetypes

Your result places you on this arc

From broadcasting at people to building with them.

Stage 01

Broadcast Organization

Talks at an audience. Reach is high, return is low. Growth depends entirely on spend.

Stage 02

Passive Community

People gather but rarely act. Presence without participation. A audience waiting to be activated.

Stage 03

Awakening Brand

The first real loops appear. Some users return on their own. The system starts to pull its weight.

Stage 04

Participation System

Contribution is designed in. Users build, return and invest. Retention compounds without constant spend.

Stage 05

Movement Builder

Belonging becomes identity. The community carries the mission — and your most credible growth comes from them.
